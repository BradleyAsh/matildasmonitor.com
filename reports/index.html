Individual player reports

One player, one match, usually the full 90 minutes. Reports are numbered in the order they were published.

How tiers are assigned. Tier 1: uncapped, aged 15 to 22, on the pathway (NPLW, academy or A-League Women). Tier 2: emerging A-League Women players aged 18 to 25, uncapped or with only a handful of senior caps. Tier 3: the overseas pipeline, footage permitting. Club, competition, age and cap status are as at the date of the report, not today. The tiers were formalised in 2026; earlier reports are classified retrospectively against the same rules, a senior Matilda sits outside them, and a player under 15 is marked Pathway (U15) rather than forced into Tier 1. Where a report did not state an age, year of birth is shown instead.
